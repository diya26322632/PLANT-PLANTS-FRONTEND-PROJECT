OUR STORY

We started our store in 30th June 2021 .Our store is known as Plant Plants in Patiala ,Punjab . So we take the initiative to bring our business online ,so more and more people will take the benefits. We sell different different types of plants all over in Punjab viva online or offline .

PLANT PLANTS is managed by :-

A team of 14 wonderful staff who are experts at growing “Happy Plants”. Everything is fabricated, improvised and managed inhouse. There are no boundaries, everything is managed by anyone who is at hand. A retired Gunner who thought it would be easy to convert from ‘Arms to Farms’. When not playing with all kinds of pets and animals, he just watches the plants grow. Well some consider it work and some consider it as enjoyment!! And if you are not happy with anything , you can aim all your missiles at him. There are other pillars propping up Plant , but they wish to remain in the background, with Diya as the first line of defence.
